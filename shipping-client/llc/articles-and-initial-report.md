# Articles of Organization and Initial Report: what goes in every field

Louisiana files these two together, online at geauxBIZ (the Secretary of State's portal), with the registered agent's notarized acceptance. The name must be reserved on geauxBIZ first. Fees since October 1, 2026: $125 for the Articles, plus the portal's online service charge; the annual report is $35 a year on the anniversary. Confirm on the Secretary of State's fee page the day you file.

## Right after filing

1. **EIN** from the IRS at irs.gov, free, same day: choose "LLC", three members, taxed as a partnership.
2. **Bank account** in the company's name with the Articles, the EIN letter and the operating agreement.
3. **Louisiana Revenue** registration through geauxBIZ or LaTAP for any tax the accountant says applies (a software license fee to the store is a service; a sold copy of software can be taxable, which matters only when PeterShip is sold to another store).
4. **Update the license file** copyright line once the IP Assignment is signed (see that draft, Section 4).
5. **Put the anniversary in the calendar** for the annual report.
