# Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement

**Draft, October 29, 2026. Not legal advice. A Louisiana attorney reviews this before signing.**

This Agreement is made as of [date, after the Company exists] by:

- Peter Ho, domiciled in Ouachita Parish, Louisiana,
- Rin Ho, domiciled in Ouachita Parish, Louisiana, and
- William Parker, domiciled in East Carroll Parish, Louisiana

(each an "Assignor", together the "Assignors"), in favour of PeterShip, LLC, a Louisiana limited liability company (the "Company").

## Background

The Assignors are the "Owners" named in the PeterShip Proprietary License, version 1.1, dated October 26, 2026, and together hold all rights in the software it describes. They are forming the Company to own and commercialise that software, and each is contributing their rights to it as part of their membership interest under the Company's Operating Agreement.

## 1. What is assigned

1.1 Each Assignor assigns to the Company all of their right, title and interest, worldwide, in the "Assigned Property", which is:
  (a) the software known as PeterShip as described in Section 1 of the PeterShip Proprietary License, including everything under `shipping-client/`, `site/src/shipping/`, the PeterShip pages, routes, tables, workflows and configuration named there, and every later file marked as part of PeterShip;
  (b) the public API, the Shopify connector, the printer agent, the batch, pickup, claims, mailbox, returns, account and card-order code added to the repository `wbp318/goin-postal-forsythe` through the date of this Agreement, to the extent it is not already in (a);
  (c) all documentation, designs, plans, tests, data models, diagrams and research written for the above;
  (d) the name "PeterShip" and any logo, domain or mark used with it;
  (e) all copyrights, trade secrets, know-how, and any patent rights in the above, all registrations and applications, and all rights to sue for past infringement.
1.2 Not assigned: the Goin' Postal name and marks (GP Brands'), the store's own data (customers, shipments, prices), the repository's website pages outside PeterShip, and anything under the repository's root Apache and Creative Commons licenses that is not PeterShip.

## 2. Consideration

The assignment is made in exchange for each Assignor's membership interest in the Company under the Operating Agreement, which each Assignor acknowledges is sufficient.

## 3. Promises

3.1 Each Assignor states that they have the right to make this assignment, have not assigned or licensed the Assigned Property to anyone else except under the PeterShip Proprietary License, and know of no claim against it.
3.2 Each Assignor will sign any further document the Company reasonably needs to record or defend the assignment, at the Company's cost, and appoints the Company's Manager as attorney-in-fact to sign such documents if the Assignor cannot be reached within thirty days.
3.3 Any moral rights an Assignor may have in the Assigned Property are waived in favour of the Company to the extent the law allows.

## 4. The license file

On signing, the Company becomes the sole "Owner" under the PeterShip Proprietary License. The Company will update the copyright line of `shipping-client/LICENSE` to read "Copyright (c) 2026 PeterShip, LLC, Ouachita Parish, Louisiana" and the Assignors consent to that change.

## 5. Future work

Work on the Assigned Property by any Assignor after this date is work for the Company under Section 8 of the Operating Agreement and is owned by the Company as created, without further assignment.

## 6. General

Louisiana law governs. This is the whole agreement about the assignment. Counterparts and electronic signatures are fine.
