# Operating Agreement of PeterShip, LLC

**Draft, October 29, 2026. Not legal advice. A Louisiana attorney reviews this before signing.** Blanks in [brackets] are the members' decisions; see the README.

This Operating Agreement (the "Agreement") of PeterShip, LLC (the "Company"), a Louisiana limited liability company, is made as of [date] by its members:

- Peter Ho, domiciled in Ouachita Parish, Louisiana
- Rin Ho, domiciled in Ouachita Parish, Louisiana
- William Parker, domiciled in East Carroll Parish, Louisiana

(each a "Member", together the "Members").

## 1. Formation

1.1 The Company was formed under the Louisiana Limited Liability Company Law (La. R.S. 12:1301 and following) by Articles of Organization filed with the Louisiana Secretary of State on [date].
1.2 The Company's name is PeterShip, LLC. Its registered office and principal office are at 1800 Forsythe Ave Ste 2, Monroe, Louisiana 71201, or wherever the Manager later decides and reports to the State.
1.3 The Company's registered agent is [Peter Ho at the registered office], who has accepted the appointment in writing.
1.4 The Company lasts until it is dissolved under Section 11.

## 2. Purpose

2.1 The Company develops, owns, licenses, sells and supports software for shipping, mailbox, printing and related retail businesses, beginning with the software known as PeterShip, and may do anything lawful that serves that purpose.

## 3. Members, interests and contributions

3.1 Each Member's membership interest, capital contribution and share of profits and losses is:

| Member | Interest | Initial contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Peter Ho | [33.34]% | [describe: cash, services, the store's cooperation] |
| Rin Ho | [33.33]% | [describe: cash] |
| William Parker | [33.33]% | [describe: assignment of the PeterShip code under the IP Assignment, valued at $[ ]] |

3.2 Each Member assigns to the Company all of their rights in the PeterShip software under the IP Assignment Agreement signed with this Agreement, as part of their contribution.
3.3 No Member must contribute more. Extra contributions need all Members' consent and are recorded in a revised Section 3.1.
3.4 Interests are personal property. A Member has no interest in any specific Company asset.

## 4. Management

4.1 The Company is manager-managed. The Manager is Peter Ho. A Manager serves until they resign, die, become unable to act, or are removed by Members holding more than two thirds of the interests, who then appoint a replacement.
4.2 The Manager runs the ordinary business: contracts under $[5,000], hiring contractors, the Company's accounts and filings, the software license to the Forsythe store, support and pricing to customers.
4.3 These need the written consent of all Members:
  (a) selling the Company, or all or most of its assets, or merging it;
  (b) licensing PeterShip to anyone other than Goin' Postal Forsythe, or granting exclusivity to anyone;
  (c) borrowing or guaranteeing more than $[5,000], or pledging Company assets;
  (d) admitting a new Member, or changing any Member's interest;
  (e) changing this Agreement or the Articles;
  (f) dissolving the Company;
  (g) any transaction between the Company and a Member or a Member's business (including the Forsythe store) other than the software license at Section 9.
4.4 Members act by written consent, which may be by email, signed or acknowledged by each consenting Member. No meetings are required; any Member may call one with seven days' notice.
4.5 William Parker is the Company's lead developer. Code he writes for the Company is the Company's under the IP Assignment and Section 8, whether or not he is paid for it.

## 5. Profits, losses and distributions

5.1 Profits and losses are allocated by interest.
5.2 The Manager distributes cash the Company does not need for operations and a reserve of $[ ] at least once a year, by interest. The Company makes a tax distribution each year sufficient for each Member's tax on their allocated income, at the highest combined marginal rate, unless the Members waive it.
5.3 No distribution may leave the Company unable to pay its debts as they fall due.

## 6. Books, accounts and tax

6.1 The Company keeps its books on the calendar year, on the cash or accrual basis the accountant recommends, at the principal office, open to every Member.
6.2 The Company is taxed as a partnership. The Manager is the partnership representative. Any election to be taxed as a corporation needs all Members' consent.
6.3 Each Member gets a K-1 within ninety days of year end.

## 7. Liability and indemnity

7.1 No Member or Manager is personally liable for the Company's debts.
7.2 The Company indemnifies a Member or Manager for acts in good faith on the Company's behalf, except for fraud, wilful misconduct, a knowing violation of law, or a transaction from which they took an improper personal benefit.

## 8. Intellectual property and confidentiality

8.1 All software, documentation, designs, data models, names and marks created for the Company by any Member or contractor belong to the Company. Each Member assigns such work to the Company as it is created.
8.2 Members keep the Company's source code, customer data, pricing and plans confidential, during membership and for three years after, except what is public or what the law requires.
8.3 A Member who leaves keeps no license to PeterShip.

## 9. The Forsythe store

9.1 The Company licenses PeterShip to Goin' Postal Forsythe (owned by [Peter Ho / the store's entity]) under the Software License Agreement signed with this Agreement, at $[1] a year, non-exclusive. The store's data belongs to the store.
9.2 Because Peter Ho is both Manager and the store's owner, the license and any change to it are approved by Rin Ho and William Parker.

## 10. Transfers of interests

10.1 A Member may not sell, give or pledge their interest without the other Members' consent, except to a trust for the Member's family with notice.
10.2 Right of first refusal: a Member wishing to sell first offers the interest to the Company, then to the other Members by interest, at the same price and terms as the outside offer, for sixty days.
10.3 On a Member's death or incapacity, their successor receives the economic rights only; admission as a Member needs the other Members' consent.
10.4 Any transfer against this Section is void.

## 11. Dissolution

11.1 The Company dissolves on all Members' consent, or when the law requires.
11.2 On dissolution the Manager winds up: pays creditors, sets reserves, and distributes the rest to Members by their capital accounts and then by interest. The PeterShip code and name are sold or distributed as the Members decide; absent agreement, each Member receives a non-exclusive copy and the Company's name is retired.

## 12. General

12.1 Louisiana law governs. Disputes go first to mediation in Ouachita Parish; then to the courts of Ouachita Parish.
12.2 This Agreement is the whole agreement among the Members about the Company and replaces the ownership statements in the PeterShip license file once the IP Assignment is signed.
12.3 Changes must be in writing, signed by all Members.
12.4 If a part is unenforceable, the rest stands.
12.5 Counterparts and electronic signatures are fine.
