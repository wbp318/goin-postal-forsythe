Check first: is the store registered as a CMRA? A store that rents mailboxes is a Commercial Mail Receiving Agency and files PS Form 1583-A with the local postmaster. Goin' Postal stores usually are already. Call or visit the Monroe post office and confirm before taking a customer.

CMRA registration (PS Form 1583-A) confirmed with the Monroe postmaster. Date: Ask too: is the store set up in USPS's online CMRA Customer Registration Database? New 1583s go in it, and the quarterly certification is done there (DMM 508 1.8.3).

A new mailbox customer

About fifteen minutes. The customer must be here in person with two IDs.

  1. Pick the size and term. Small, medium or large; 6 months or a year (12 months for the price of 10). There is no monthly term.
  2. See two IDs (page 3 lists what counts). One must have a photo. One must show the current home address. Neither may be expired. Look at them yourself; a photo on a phone does not count.
  3. Copy both IDs on the counter scanner or copier. The copies go in the locked drawer with the 1583.
  4. Fill in PS Form 1583 together. The customer completes their part and signs. You fill in the CMRA part: the box number, the two IDs you saw, and sign as the agent. Every person who will collect mail from the box goes on the form, and each shows their own two IDs.
  5. The service agreement (page 4): the customer reads and signs. You sign.
  6. Take the first payment on the POS. Write the POS receipt number on the agreement.
  7. The register: Counter tab, New customer: name, phone, email, size (S, M or L), term (6 or Y). It gives the next MB- code and jumps to the ID cells: type both IDs (type, last 4 only, expiry), the 1583 signed date and your name. Then Record a payment with the POS receipt number.
  8. Give the box. Counter tab, Give a box. It refuses until the IDs, the 1583 and the payment are in, then offers the Free boxes of their size and shows their address. Hand over two keys and have them sign the key receipt (page 5).
  9. Tell them their address and write it on the back of their receipt:
Their name or business
1800 Forsythe Ave Ste 2 #BOX
Monroe, LA 71201

USPS requires a CMRA customer's address to carry "#" or "PMB" with the box number. Mail without it can be returned to the sender. The store already has "Ste 2", so ask the postmaster once which they want: "Ste 2 #101" on one line, or "PMB 101" on its own line above the street. Then cross out the one not used here.

  1. File the paper: the 1583, the ID copies, the agreement and the key receipt, stapled, in the locked drawer, under the box number. Scan the signed 1583 too: USPS asks the store to keep a digital copy, and to enter it in the CMRA database if the store is set up there.

Closing a box

  1. Collect both keys. Hand over any mail still in the box.
  2. Ask for a forwarding address and write it on their 1583.
  3. Counter tab, Close a box: it writes today as the Closed date and the forwarding address. The box shows Free again.
  4. Keep the paper file. USPS expects the CMRA to keep the 1583 after the box closes; confirm how long with the postmaster.

Which IDs count

From PS Form 1583 (2023). Two IDs, two different kinds.

Photo IDs (one of these is required)

  • Driver's license or state ID card
  • Passport, or US passport card
  • Military or uniformed service ID
  • Government employee ID
  • University ID
  • Permanent resident card
  • Certificate of naturalization
  • Tribal ID

Second ID (or a second photo ID)

  • Current lease, mortgage or deed of trust
  • Voter registration card
  • Vehicle registration card
  • Home or vehicle insurance policy
  • Any photo ID from the list on the left, of a different kind
Never accepted: Social Security card, credit or debit card, birth certificate. Not expired. One of the two must show the customer's current home address.
