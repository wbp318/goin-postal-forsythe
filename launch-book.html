Check with corporate before any ad goes to print

Goin' Postal owns its name and logo. Franchise agreements almost always require local ads to use the official logo files and pass a brand review. Ask your franchise rep for the brand guide and the approval process before ordering banners or a billboard.

Section 2 · Local sponsorships

Moccasins and Friday nights

The Monroe Moccasins' 2026–27 season opened at the Monroe Civic Center on Oct 9 and runs into April. Upcoming home dates include Oct 17, Nov 6–7 and Nov 21, all at 7:05 PM. High school football runs through November, with basketball and baseball after. Those seasons overlap, so one sponsor package can cover most of the year.

The Moccasins don't publish sponsorship prices. Use the partner inquiry form at moccasinshockey.com/sponsors. 103 Rocks (103.1 FM) carries every game, so radio spots are another way to reach the same fans.
