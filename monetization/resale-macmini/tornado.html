Inside the Mac mini

One small Apple computer, an M4 Mac mini, runs the resale work on a schedule, day and night. It can be one of William's counter minis with extra accounts, or its own at the workroom desk; never the deposit station's sealed mini. People touch only the first layer. The other four run on their own and can't reach each other's keys.

  1. 1
    Staff account people

    The only login a person uses. Start an item from the Obsidian template, AirDrop the photos from a phone, read Claude's draft, set the price and mark it approved. Post to Poshmark and Mercari by hand in Chrome.

    Stages 1, 2, 5 · can't see the marketplace keys

  2. 2
    launchd jobs scripts

    Plain scripts the Mac starts on a timer, no AI. They move and shrink photos, check Claude's draft, publish what a person approved, and when something sells, end the other listings and buy the label through PeterShip. For dropship they read the supplier's feed (new items, stock, prices), order a sold item from the supplier and send its tracking to the marketplace.

    Stages 1 (supplier feed), 2, 4, 6, 7, 8 · the hidden lister account holds the eBay, Etsy and supplier keys

  3. 3
    Claude Code Claude

    Writes the listing draft from the photos and notes: title, description, measurements, flaws. For a dropship item, from the supplier's facts, in the store's own words. Claude Haiku 5.5 does routine items, one per run; designer brands, possible fakes and anything over $75 get a Claude Sonnet 5.5 second opinion. Also writes the 9 pm sales note and Monday's list of slow sellers.

    Stage 3 · writes only draft.md and notes · reaches api.anthropic.com and nothing else

  4. 4
    Obsidian vault the record

    A folder of notes, one per item, with its history saved every night to a private GitHub repo the partners can read from home. People write item.md, Claude writes draft.md, the scripts write listings.md.

    Stage 5 happens here · only a person sets approved

  5. 5
    The walls rules

    What keeps Claude away from money and accounts: root-owned settings nobody at the desk can change (no shell, no web, Haiku and Sonnet only, no Opus), separate macOS accounts, the sandbox, and LuLu, a free firewall that limits where each program can connect.

    Around Claude · tested by asking it to break them

The same rule everywhere: the official API with the store's own keys, a person approving every listing, no bots or browser automation, and nothing from a marketplace (buyer details included) ever goes to Claude. When the marketplace takes the payment it collects Louisiana sales tax; on the store's own shop and local cash sales, the store does.

Dropship, start to finish

The diamonds in the storm. The store lists goods it never stocks; when one sells, a script orders it from a US supplier, who ships it straight to the buyer. The store's own designs work the same way through a print partner. The store is still the seller: for eBay's standards, the FTC's shipping rule, returns and sales tax. Full plan: dropship/README.md.

One sale, step by step

  1. 1
    Feed. Every 15 to 60 minutes the supplier's API sends new items, stock and cost. Stock under 10 sets the listing to 0 at once.
  2. 2
    Draft. Claude Haiku 5.5 writes from the supplier's facts in the store's words, never copying them.
  3. 3
    Approve. A person checks it and sets the price at or above the script's floor. Listed 2 or 3 at a time.
  4. 4
    Sold. The order job checks the address, the fraud score and the live cost. Below the floor or out of stock: held for a person.
  5. 5
    Order. Through the supplier's API, shipped blind. For the first months a person confirms each one.
  6. 6
    Tracking. The supplier's webhook brings it; the job posts it to eBay (createShippingFulfillment), Shopify or Etsy within handling time.
  7. 7
    Watchdog. No tracking by handling time minus 12 hours, no scan 24 hours later, or not delivered by the estimate: a person is told.

Money: the floor

The script's floor is 15% kept after fees, ads and returns. On eBay that's a price of at least the supplier's delivered cost ÷ 0.62. A 25% markup keeps about $0.70 on this item.

eBay's 13.6% is on the total with the buyer's sales tax, plus $0.40 an order. Shopify Basic is $39 a month ($29 yearly) at 2.9% + 30¢; its cost is finding buyers. An Etsy tee of the store's design at $26 keeps about $14 through Printify.

eBay's standards

Defects (Below Standard over)2% Cases closed without the store0.3%, min 2 Late shipments (Top Rated)3% or less Tracking on time (Top Rated)95% Below Standard costs+6% fees

An out-of-stock cancel is a defect: that's why the feed lists 2 or 3 at a time and zeroes stock under 10. Handling time starts at 3 business days (5 for designs). Rated on the 20th of each month.
