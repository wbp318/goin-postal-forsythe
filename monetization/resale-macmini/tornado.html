What just happened

Mock data, from the plan in monetization/resale-macmini/: 20 items a day; about 10% flagged to Claude Sonnet 5.5 (designer brands, possible fakes, over $75), the rest drafted by Claude Haiku 5.5; about half of listed items sell within the month; marketplace fees shown as 13.5% for the example; Claude costs at the plan's estimates ($0.0065 a Haiku draft, $0.117 a Sonnet second opinion, $0.009 a daily note). eBay always; Etsy only for vintage; Poshmark, Mercari and the other by-hand marketplaces only after 14 days unsold. Nothing here is real sales data.
