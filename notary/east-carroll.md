# East Carroll Parish: notary commission from Lake Providence

For a candidate who lives in East Carroll Parish (Peter). The commission is issued for the parish of residence, which is the parish of voter registration. The store in Monroe does not change that. Once the exam is passed, the commission is good statewide (R.S. 35:191(P)), so an East Carroll notary can notarize at 1800 Forsythe Avenue every day.

Written 4 November 2026. Phone every office before driving; hours below come from directories, not the offices' own sites, and are marked (confirm).

## Step 0: voter registration

You must be registered to vote in East Carroll Parish on the day you file the Application to Qualify (R.S. 35:191(B)). Check first at the GeauxVote portal, https://voterportal.sos.la.gov. If the registration shows another parish, or an old address, fix it before applying. Registration changes take effect at once for this purpose but the application asks for the parish, so the record must already say East Carroll.

## Clerk of court: where the duplicate oath goes

After passing, the oath of office is taken before a notary and two originals are signed. One goes to the Secretary of State with the bond, the signature form and the $35 commission documents fee. The duplicate goes to the clerk of court of the parish of commission within one month of the oath. Since 1 February 2026 a surety-company bond no longer has to be approved or recorded by the clerk (R.S. 35:192(C), Act 258 of 2025); only a personal-surety bond or a special-mortgage bond still goes through the clerk's mortgage office. Buy the surety-company bond and skip that.

Ask the clerk at the same visit:

1. Whether they want the duplicate oath "filed" or "recorded" and the fee for each.
2. Whether they keep a notary register for the parish and want the signature on file.
3. The recording fee schedule for acts of sale, mortgages and donations, because R.S. 35:199 will make you file acts on East Carroll property here.
4. Whether UCC-1 filings are accepted at this office (every parish clerk is part of the central registry).

## Nearest exam site

All sittings are at LSU in Baton Rouge only (Himes Hall area, Memorial Clock Tower, accessible entrance via David Boyd Hall). From Lake Providence it is about 215 miles and 3.5 to 4 hours by car via US-65 and US-61 or I-20 and US-165 to I-49 (confirm the route). Plan on going down the night before: the exam is a four-hour sitting and check-in is early morning (the admission email, sent about five days before, gives the time). Visitor parking near the Clock Tower is paid on weekdays and generally open on Saturdays. LSU Welcome Center for directions: 225-578-5030.

## Local differences worth knowing

- East Carroll is a small parish (population under 7,000). The clerk's office is one room and one phone number; expect to be told to come in rather than mail things. Call first.
- The courthouse holds both the clerk and the registrar, so Step 0 and the oath filing are the same building.
- Because the store is in Ouachita, almost every act the notary passes at the counter will concern Ouachita property or people, and will be recorded with the Ouachita clerk (see `ouachita.md`), not here. The East Carroll clerk sees only the oath and acts about East Carroll property.
- R.S. 35:191(A)(2) lets a notary commissioned in the parish of residence also act in adjacent parishes with populations under 40,000 where the notary or employer has an office, without more. That is irrelevant for a post-2005 exam passer, who has statewide jurisdiction anyway.
- If the candidate later moves to Ouachita Parish, the commission must be moved: new voter registration, then a change-of-parish filing with the Secretary of State (R.S. 35:191(E)); no new exam.
