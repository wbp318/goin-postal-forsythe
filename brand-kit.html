01

Color

Nine values, no others. No tints, gradients or "close enough" matches. Navy is the ground, white is the label, yellow happens once.

Ground

Navy

#0F1D3A

Page ground behind every label. Big service lines, MOCS10, holiday routing block.

Mono field labels only: FROM, SHIP TO, SERVICE, PROMO CODE. Thin underlines on edit fields.

  1. 1
    FROM + logo slot

    Store name in Plex Sans 600, address underneath. Dashed OFFICIAL LOGO box top-right. A strip of kraft tape crosses the top edge or a corner.

  2. 2
    SHIP TO / edit fields

    The variable line: opponent, venue, date. Barlow 600 on a thin postal-blue underline at 50% opacity. Mark it "EDIT ·" in small mono at the right.

  3. 3
    Yellow band

    Full label width, edge to edge between two rules. One per piece. One or two words of Barlow 800 in yellow ink, fitted to about 92% of the width.

  4. 4
    Tracking barcode + code

    Ink bars of 4/8/12 units on a 4-unit grid, with a mono tracking line under it. Decorative only, not scannable. The promo code sits beside it in navy.

  5. 5
    Services

    The fixed footer: three services in Barlow 800, carriers in mono. Same words on every piece.

  6. —
    Rules & margins

    Rules are solid ink, about 0.5% of artboard width (6px on 1080). The label sits at least 4% in from every edge of the navy ground, and no element crosses a rule.

04

The OFFICIAL LOGO slot

These files never draw a logo. Every ad carries a dashed box marked OFFICIAL LOGO. The real Goin' Postal corporate logo goes in that box, and only there.

OFFICIAL LOGO
OFFICIAL
LOGO
OFFICIAL LOGO

Wide · square · reversed on navy2–3px dash, ink or white

How to fill it

  • Use the corporate logo file supplied by Goin' Postal franchise support. Do not redraw, recolor, stretch or retype it.
  • Get brand approval from the franchisor before anything prints or goes on a board.
  • Fit the logo inside the dashed box with clear space on all sides, then delete the dashed outline and the placeholder text.
  • Team, school and venue names stay plain text. Never place a team, school or league logo or mascot unless that organization supplies and approves it.

Copy rules

Short, plain, and the same every time. The ads sell three services at one corner of Monroe.

Never on an ad

  • No phone numbers. The counter flyer has a blank PHONE line to write in by hand.
  • No prices and no "starting at" amounts.
  • No hours. They change; leave a blank HOURS line instead.
  • No services beyond the three above, and no carriers beyond the four.
  • Store name as text: Goin' Postal · Forsythe. The logo lives only in the slot.
  • In SVG source, write the ampersand as &amp;.
MOCS10

Coupon terms · always all three

Wherever the Moccasins code appears it is MOCS10, followed by "One per customer" and "Name + phone required". If there is no room for the terms, there is no room for the code.

06

Legibility by medium

Distance and seconds decide the word count. The further away the viewer, the fewer words and the bigger the yellow.

≤ 7words of headline
on a billboard
  • Read at 55 mph in about 5 seconds. Headline plus location, nothing else large.
  • Smallest text at least 1/30 of board height.
  • No code, no terms, no fine print.
1 linearena boards,
fence banners
  • Seen across a rink or field and on TV. One message, read left to right.
  • Text at least 40% of board height. Nothing in the bottom 10% (kick plates, grass).
  • Fence banners: keep copy out of the grommet margin.
8 secLED & scoreboard
spots, static
  • LED glows: avoid thin mono lines on navy at small size; go one weight heavier.
  • No hairlines under 4px at final pixel size; they shimmer.
  • Deliver at the exact pixel size the operator gives you.
07

File formats

The SVGs are the masters. Everything else is exported from them, never edited separately.

Source

SVG

Edit these. Open in Illustrator, Inkscape, Affinity or any browser. Text stays live and editable.

Sharing

PNG

For WhatsApp, texts, email, social and LED operators. Exported to designs/png/.

Print

PDF

For the print shop. The counter flyer exports at 8.5×11 in to designs/pdf/.

# from the repo root, online so Google Fonts load (needs Chrome or Edge) python scripts/export_designs.py # options python scripts/export_designs.py --out DIR --browser PATH

Before anything prints

  • Either install the three fonts (free from Google Fonts) on the machine that opens the SVG, or outline the text (convert to paths) in the PDF you send.
  • Without them the file falls back to Arial Narrow or Impact and line lengths change.
  • Ask for a proof. Check the yellow and the navy on the actual material.

Sizing for vinyl and boards

  • SVG is vector, so scale it to any size; keep the aspect ratio of the artboard.
  • Add bleed in your layout app. The 4% margin inside the art is the safe zone.
  • Keep the original SVG next to every export so the next edit starts from source.
